# Nell — Ecosystem & Funding

## Mission

Bring in adopters, mentors and money, and turn adopters into contributors.

## Objectives and KPIs

| Objective | KPI |
| --- | --- |
| Adopters are visible | Adopter case studies drafted and published |
| Mentoring capacity | GSoC (or similar) mentoring plan and applications filed |
| Funding | Grant applications drafted and submitted |
| Adopters contribute | Adopters with at least one maintainer-hour a month |

1. Keep an adopter list with evidence and a contact route (owner-approved only).
2. Draft case studies with Juno.
3. Track mentoring programme and grant deadlines; draft applications early.
